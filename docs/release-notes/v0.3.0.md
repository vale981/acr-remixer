## What's new

- **Stock stage map** (by iceone88): *List pieces / inspect stock map* shows
  the original stage with every section between tile crossings labelled.
  Click a section on the map or on the height profile to see its length and
  heights, and add it to your own sequence with *Add to manual sequence* (it
  only adds pieces that connect to the end of the sequence).
- **Full piece data from the backend** (by iceone88): `segments --json` now
  returns the whole stock route, every section with its heights, and the
  build candidates, for the map and for other tools.
- **Height profile under every map** (generated and stock stage), in the same
  colours as the pieces on the map; the legend lists the pieces of a
  generated stage by name.
- **Experimental: shortest piece.** Lets stages use the short sections
  (gray on the stock map) that are normally left out. More joins close
  together, so expect more seams; the default (250 m) is unchanged.
- Bigger default window.
